HIKING From R150

Table Mountain Trail Hike

Cape Town, Western Cape
4–6 hours Moderate

Experience the iconic Table Mountain on foot. Choose from multiple trails offering panoramic views of Cape Town, the Atlantic Seaboard, and the Cape Peninsula.

KAYAKING From R280

Knysna Lagoon Kayak Tour

Knysna, Garden Route
2–3 hours Easy

Paddle through the calm, turquoise waters of the Knysna Lagoon, surrounded by lush indigenous forest and the famous Knysna Heads.

ZIP-LINING From R450

Tsitsikamma Forest Zip-line

Tsitsikamma, Eastern Cape
3 hours Easy

Soar through the ancient Tsitsikamma forest canopy on 10 interconnected zip-lines, taking in stunning gorge views and indigenous forest biodiversity.

ROCK CLIMBING From R200

Cederberg Rock Climbing

Rocklands, Western Cape
Full day Challenging

Scale world-renowned sandstone formations in the Cederberg Wilderness. From beginner boulders to advanced multi-pitch routes, the Cederberg delivers unforgettable climbing.

MOUNTAIN BIKING From R350

Cape Winelands Mountain Bike

Stellenbosch, Western Cape
4–5 hours Moderate

Ride through world-class wine farms and forested mountain trails in the Cape Winelands. Single-track singletracks, river crossings, and vineyard vistas await.

Orange River Rafting

Augrabies, Northern Cape
